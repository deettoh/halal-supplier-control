## Grounding

Mechanism: one supplier renewal updates the record used to calculate coverage for multiple linked SMEs; delivery labels are read, compared by deterministic rules, and exceptions reach a supervisor with evidence.

Scene: a micro F&B team's receiving door and office, shifting between phone photography, supplier records and an audit-ready decision. The first landing viewport must make that mechanism and an Enter demo action understandable. Its appearance can be expressive; operational screens must remain easy to scan.

Avoid the common navy SaaS dashboard with icon cards and the predictable opposite of a cream editorial page with thin rules, serif display and decorative stamps. The old screenshots are a styling anti-reference. No generic gradients, fake KPI blocks, official seals, invented testimonials or compliance guarantees.

Seven grounded systems considered, in initial resonance order:

1. Receiving-counter dockets: decisive material names, expected-versus-observed information and a clear next action.
2. Supplier catalogue index: one product identity connected to its current record, with clear versions and references.
3. Food-supply trade bulletin: a concrete receiving story and explanatory product imagery, with an operational reading order.
4. Loading-bay wayfinding: confident type and routing that connects receiving, supervisor and supplier without hidden navigation.
5. Ingredient packaging systems: product/brand/manufacturer hierarchy grounded in the actual thing being photographed.
6. Stock-control terminal: predictable rows, immediate exception signals and explicit transaction outcomes.
7. Reconciliation ledger: aligned records expose what matches, what changed and which shared certificate each buyer uses.

## Challenger judgments and retained disciplines

Judged on audience identification and product clarity before borrowing any discipline:

- Luminescent forest — declined on both axes. A growing network does not explain business records or staff actions. Shared Ledger retains its discipline of one unambiguous focal state, without glow or botanical imagery.
- Iridescent cloud edge — declined on both axes. Spectral ambiguity does not help someone distinguish coverage from missing evidence. Shared Ledger retains a clearly labelled uncertain state that never resembles success.
- Printing darkroom — competitive on product clarity through label-photo evidence; weaker on audience identification than actual business records. Retained as the full Evidence Room alternate.
- Exposure-record overlays — declined on both axes. Overlapping hatches conceal precisely the evidence the supervisor needs. Shared Ledger adopts strict tonal grouping while keeping all photo content unobstructed.
- Painted film poster — declined on both axes. A metaphorical image cannot carry repeated material/record comparisons clearly. Shared Ledger retains the discipline of a single dominant thesis rather than several competing hero features.
- Matchbook drawer — declined on both axes. Flipping miniature covers obscures expiry/scope and long audit entries. Shared Ledger retains craftsmanship at small control sizes without foil, nostalgia or hidden facts.

The category-standard exit is omitted from the comparison because the user explicitly requested not to see conventional AI-style design. This follows the user's preference over the skill's default offer. The user can request a new direction or a revision in ordinary language.
