Loupe — Focus Ribbon identity

The ribbon is an original SVG redraw of the team's selected logo concept.
The Loupe wordmark is outlined from Public Sans, weight 900, distributed under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1. See /fonts/Public-Sans-OFL.txt for the full license.
The application does not need to download a font to render this identity.

The wordmark and symbol communicate close inspection of label evidence.
They do not designate a certificate issuer or independent certifying authority.
